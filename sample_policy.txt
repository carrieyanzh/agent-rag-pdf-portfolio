ACME CORP INTERNAL POLICY DOCUMENT (REVISION 2026)
1. HOLIDAY ALLOWANCE: Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave. 
Unused leave up to 5 days can be carried over to the next financial year.
2. REMOTE WORK BENEFIT: Employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week, 
subject to manager approval. Core office hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
3. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT: Monthly internet stipends for remote workers are capped at $50 USD. 
All expense claims must be submitted by the 25th day of the current month.
